ClearPath Blocker

Blocks adult sites and keywords, forces SafeSearch on Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo, and puts YouTube in Restricted Mode. Pausing protection requires the lock password.

Accountability lock

Hand the keyboard to someone you trust (spouse, friend) and have them type a password you won't know. Pausing protection — even for 30 minutes — requires this password.

Pause protection (30 minutes)

Requires the lock password. Protection resumes automatically.

Make it tamper-resistant

  • This extension can be uninstalled from chrome://extensions — that's the weak point of any self-installed blocker.
  • Pair it with DNS filtering (NextDNS or CleanBrowsing) set at the router, with the account password held by your accountability partner.
  • On iPhone, use Screen Time → Limit Adult Websites with a passcode someone else sets.
